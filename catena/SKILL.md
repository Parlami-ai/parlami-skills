# 🧮 BACKLINK QUALITY SCORE (BQS)

BQS =
(Geo Relevance × 2) +
(Topical Relevance × 2) +
(Domain Authority ÷ 5) +
(Traffic Score ÷ 10) +
(Link Placement Score) +
(Media Authority Bonus)

**Media Authority Bonus:**
- Newspaper/TV site: +20
- Magazine: +15
- Popular blog (DA 30+): +10
- Podcast show notes: +5
- Association/directory: +5

Only pursue prospects with BQS ≥ 50 (lowered from 60 to include emerging bloggers).

## 📚 Operational Knowledge (Distilled from Training)

### Key Rules — Earned Media & Link Building
1. **Personalization is non-negotiable:** Generic mass pitches die in inbox. Name the reporter, reference their recent work, explain why YOUR story fits THEIR beat specifically.
2. **In-person ask at service moment > email/text by 10x:** "Hope your child had a great day! Would mean a lot if you could leave us a review mentioning their favorite activity."
3. **Relationship-first, pitch-second:** Contact journalists when you DON'T need coverage. Share useful articles, congratulate on published work. Become a source, not just a requester.
4. **Multimedia 72% higher open rate:** Pitches with photos/videos/infographics open more. Prepare media kit: high-res photos, video clips, pull quotes, key stats, easy-to-copy format.
5. **Speed is essential:** HARO/Connectively responses must be <30 minutes. First responders get priority. Pre-write expert quote templates for fast turnaround.
6. **Community partnerships = local backlinks:** Partner with Phoenix Children's Museum, Arcadia libraries, local parent groups. Offer free workshops, co-host events. Result: backlink + goodwill.
7. **Hyperlocal content attracts natural backlinks:** "Best Family Activities in Arcadia 2026" gets referenced by community sites. Evergreen local SEO content is linkable asset.
8. **Vendor testimonials = easy backlinks:** Provide testimonials to food suppliers, cleaning services, Montessori material vendors. Often includes backlink to your site.
9. **Date-check EVERYTHING:** HARO queries have 24-72h window (7 days MAX). Stale leads (>14 days) = waste of time unless verified still active.
10. **Track all directory listings:** NAP consistency matters. Use spreadsheet: Google, Yelp, Facebook, Apple Maps, Bing, BBB, GreatSchools, Niche, Phoenix Chamber.

### Anti-Patterns — PR Pitfalls
- ❌ **Never send AI-written pitches without personalization** — Journalists spot AI instantly. Use AI for research, write final pitch personally.
- ❌ **Never pitch without researching the reporter's beat** — Sending Montessori story to tech reporter = instant delete + burned bridge.
- ❌ **Never ignore negative press** — Silence = guilt. Respond promptly, empathetically, transparently. Have crisis comms plan ready BEFORE you need it.
- ❌ **Never promise what you can't deliver:** If you say "I can get you 3 parent interviews by tomorrow," DELIVER or lose credibility forever.
- ❌ **Never review-gate:** Filtering positive reviews to Google, negative elsewhere violates guidelines. Accept all reviews publicly.
- ❌ **Never fake testimonials or reviews:** LLMs detect fake sentiment patterns. Authenticity > volume.
- ❌ **Never build spammy directory links:** 80 low-quality citations < 10 high-authority local links. Quality over quantity.
- ❌ **Never launch media kit without Maestro's approval:** Photos of kids require signed photo releases. Legal compliance first.
- ❌ **Never claim "exclusive" if you're pitching multiple outlets simultaneously:** Burns trust permanently.
- ❌ **Never pitch promotional content as news:** "Schedule a tour!" ≠ story. Human interest angles only.
