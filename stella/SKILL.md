# 🗺️ LOCAL SEO STRATEGY — The Three Pillars of Map Pack Domination

## How Google Ranks the Map Pack

**Google's Local Pack (the 3 listings shown with the map) uses THREE ranking factors:**

### Pillar 1: PROXIMITY (Distance from Searcher)
**What:** How close is the school to the person searching?
**What we control:** Nothing. We can't move the school.
**What we CAN do:**
- Ensure address is EXACTLY correct in GMB (even a wrong suite number hurts)
- Set accurate service area (not too broad, not too narrow)
- Build landing pages for surrounding neighborhoods (Zona's job, but our NAP supports it)

### Pillar 2: RELEVANCE (Does our listing match the search?)
**What:** How well does our GMB profile match what the parent searched?
**What we control:** EVERYTHING.
- Primary and secondary categories (critical — see Profile Optimization below)
- Business description with target keywords
- Services listed
- Attributes checked
- Q&A content matching common search queries
- Google Posts with relevant keywords

### Pillar 3: PROMINENCE (Is this school well-known/trusted?)
**What:** How "important" does Google think this school is?
**What we control:**
- Review count and rating (more reviews = more prominent)
- Review velocity (steady stream > burst then silence)
- GMB posting frequency (active business = prominent)
- Citations across the web (consistent NAP = trusted entity)
- Backlinks to website (Catena's domain)
- Media mentions (Catena's PR work)

## Monthly Map Pack Audit

**For each school, check:**
1. Search "[school type] [neighborhood]" on Google Maps
2. Are we in the top 3 (Map Pack)? If not, which position?
3. Who IS in the top 3? Why? (more reviews? better categories? closer to centroid?)
4. Feed findings to Zona + Spia

# 🔧 LISTING TROUBLESHOOTING — Handle GMB Problems

## Beibei's 7 Duplicate Listings

**Step-by-step resolution:**
1. **Identify the primary listing** — which has the most reviews? Which is verified? Which has correct info?
2. **Claim the primary** (if not already claimed)
3. **For each duplicate:**
   a. Open Google Maps → Search for duplicate → click "Suggest an edit"
   b. Select "Close or remove" → "Duplicate of another place"
   c. Provide the primary listing as the correct one
4. **If duplicates persist after 30 days:**
   a. Go to Google Business Profile Help → "Contact us"
   b. Select "Fix issues with your listing" → "Remove a duplicate"
   c. Provide: Primary listing ID, duplicate listing IDs, proof of ownership
5. **Track progress monthly** — some may take 60-90 days

## Profile Suspension (If It Happens)

**Common causes for education businesses:**
- Address doesn't match official records
- Name includes keywords (stuffing — "{{SCHOOL2_NAME}} - Best Preschool Phoenix")
- Multiple listings at same address
- Virtual office or PO box used

**Reinstatement process:**
1. Read the suspension email carefully (specific reason given)
2. Fix the violation (remove keyword stuffing, verify address)
3. Submit reinstatement appeal via GMB dashboard
4. Provide supporting documents (business license, utility bill, lease)
5. Wait 3-7 business days
6. If denied → escalate via Google Business Profile Forum (top contributors can help)
7. Last resort: Create new listing (loses reviews — absolute last option)

## Reporting Fake/Spam Competitor Listings

**Signs of spam:**
- Keyword-stuffed business name ("Best Cheapest Preschool Phoenix AZ")
- Address that's a residential home with no signs
- Duplicate listings for same business
- Reviews that look fake (generic, no details, burst of 10 in one day)

**How to report:**
1. Google Maps → Find competitor listing → "Suggest an edit" → "Close or remove" → "Spam"
2. Use Google Business Profile Redressal Form (for egregious cases)
3. Document everything with screenshots
4. Don't obsess over this — focus on being better, not policing others

# ⭐ REVIEW GENERATION STRATEGY

## When to Ask for Reviews

**Ask AFTER these positive touchpoints:**
1. **Post-Tour (enthusiastic parents)** — Lettera's follow-up email includes review request
2. **First Month of Enrollment (happy families)** — Lettera sends review request
3. **Parent-Teacher Conferences (positive feedback)** — Teachers ask in person
4. **Graduations/Milestones** — Celebrate + request review
5. **Parents Night Out Events** — Follow-up email with review request

**Never ask:**
- Parents who complained or had issues
- Parents who just toured but haven't enrolled yet (too soon)
- Within first 2 weeks of enrollment (not enough experience)

## Review Velocity Engineering

**Goal:** Consistent 5+ reviews per month, avoiding "feast or famine" patterns.

**Strategy:**
- **Week 1:** Request from 3 enrolled families (Lettera email)
- **Week 2:** Request from 2 recent tour families who expressed enthusiasm
- **Week 3:** Request from 3 families who've been enrolled 3+ months (proven happy)
- **Week 4:** Request from 2 families attending recent event

**Track in `review-requests-log.md`:**
- Who was asked, when, via what channel
- Who responded (to identify high-responder segments)
- Response rate by segment (tours vs enrolled vs events)

## How to Ask (Make It Easy)

**Email Template (Lettera sends this):**
```
Subject: We'd love your feedback!

Hi [Parent Name],

We're so grateful to have [Child Name] in our [program] class. Seeing them thrive in [Mandarin/Spanish/Trilingual] immersion has been wonderful!

If you have a moment, we'd love if you could share your experience on Google. Your feedback helps other families discover the gift of language learning.

👉 [Direct Google Review Link]

Thank you for being part of our [School Name] family!

[School Director Name]
```

**In-Person Ask (Teachers):**
"We're so glad [Child] is thriving! If you're happy with our program, would you mind leaving us a Google review? It really helps other families find us."

**Text Message (SMS via Lettera):**

## 📚 Operational Knowledge (Distilled from Training)

### Key Rules — Reviews Drive Everything
1. **Review recency > review count (2026 critical):** Algorithm change = fresh reviews matter more. Target: last review <7 days ago for each location.
2. **In-person ask beats digital 10x:** "Hope [child's name] had a great day! A quick Google review mentioning their favorite activity would mean a lot." Personal ask at pickup = highest conversion.
3. **Reviews train AI recommendations:** LLMs (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) analyze sentiment, authenticity, recency to recommend businesses. Detailed > generic.
4. **Respond to ALL reviews in 24-48 hours:** Positive needs reinforcement (not just "thanks"), negative needs empathy + resolution. 89% of consumers prefer businesses that respond.
5. **Mine reviews for keywords:** Parents write how they search. "Loving Mandarin teachers," "hands-on Montessori" = exact phrases for website/ads. Export, analyze, apply.
6. **Multi-platform mandatory:** Google, Yelp, Facebook all feed AI systems. NAP consistency critical. Don't focus only on Google.
7. **Never review-gate:** Filtering positive to Google, negative elsewhere = violation. Accept all reviews publicly. Authenticity > perfection.
8. **Ask specific questions:** "What did your child enjoy most?" > "Leave a review." Specific prompts generate detailed, keyword-rich reviews.
9. **GBP is your new homepage (post-AI):** Reviews display prominently in Knowledge Panel. Parents decide WITHOUT visiting website.
10. **Rich reviews with photos/videos:** Visual reviews get more engagement and weight. Encourage parents to share classroom moments (with permission).

### Anti-Patterns — Reputation Killers
- ❌ **Never incentivize reviews:** No discounts, gifts, contest entries. Google detects and penalizes. Violates FTC guidelines too.
- ❌ **Never respond angry to negatives:** Wait, cool down, respond professionally. Personal attacks in responses destroy reputation.
- ❌ **Never use template responses:** "Thank you for your feedback" 50 times = robotic. Personalize with reviewer's name, specific experience details.
- ❌ **Never ignore old unresponded reviews:** Go back, respond to ALL historical reviews. Shows you care even retroactively.
- ❌ **Never buy fake reviews:** AI + Google detect patterns. One scandal destroys years of reputation. Fake reviews get flagged by parents too.
- ❌ **Never hide from negative feedback:** Addressing concerns publicly shows confidence. Moving all discussion private looks shady.
- ❌ **Never delay responses during crisis:** If multiple bad reviews hit, respond within hours, not days. Speed contains damage.
- ❌ **Never share private information:** "Actually, your child was expelled for..." = lawsuit + reputation suicide. Stay professional.
- ❌ **Never ask at bad moments:** Don't request reviews after incidents, during transitions, or when parent seems stressed.
- ❌ **Never give up after one ask:** Follow-up via email/text 24-48 hours later with direct link. Gentle reminder, not pushy.
