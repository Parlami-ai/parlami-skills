## Headlines (max 30 characters each, 15 per asset group)

**Formula:** Emotional Hook + Benefit + Social Proof + Urgency

**Mix Template:**
1. **Emotional (3)** — "Give Them a Head Start", "Where Curiosity Thrives", "Nurture Their Potential"
2. **Benefit (5)** — "3 Languages, 1 School", "Montessori From 6 Weeks", "Trilingual Toddler Care"
3. **Social Proof (4)** — "Trusted by 100+ Families", "Award-Winning Montessori", "5-Star Rated School"
4. **Urgency/CTA (3)** — "Limited Spots – Enroll Now", "Schedule a Tour Today", "Check Availability"

**ALWAYS include:**
- Location in 3+ headlines: "{{SCHOOL2_NEIGHBORHOOD}} Montessori", "{{SCHOOL1_NEIGHBORHOOD}} Preschool"
- Trilingual differentiator in 2+ headlines: "3 Languages", "Trilingual Montessori"
- At least ONE headline with the top converting keyword for that asset group

**Character count rule:** Google REJECTS over-limit copy. Count before submitting.

## Descriptions (max 90 characters each, 4 per asset group)

**Formula:** USP + Location + CTA

**Template:**
1. "Phoenix's only trilingual Montessori. Mandarin, Spanish & English. Schedule a tour!"
2. "Full immersion from 6 weeks. Certified Montessori in {{SCHOOL2_NEIGHBORHOOD}}. Enroll today."
3. "Give your child the gift of 3 languages. {{SCHOOL2_NEIGHBORHOOD}}'s premier Montessori. Book now."
4. "Montessori meets language immersion. Ages 6wk-K2. ESA approved. Visit us!"

**ALWAYS include:**
- The school's core language differentiator (e.g. "trilingual", "bilingual", "immersion")
- Location ({{SCHOOL2_NEIGHBORHOOD}}, {{SCHOOL1_NEIGHBORHOOD}}, Phoenix)
- Strong CTA (Schedule, Enroll, Book, Visit, Check)

## Tone & Voice

**Trustworthy + Educational + Warm**
- Speak to parents' aspirations (not fear)
- Emphasize benefits (what child GAINS), not features (what school HAS)
- Use active voice: "Give your child..." not "Your child will receive..."
- Warm but not overly emotional: think professor, not carnival barker

**Words that convert:**
- Nurturing, thriving, potential, foundation, confidence, curiosity, discovery, growth
- Certified, award-winning, trusted, premier, expert, proven

**Words to avoid:**
- Daycare (in branded copy — OK in headlines if it's the search term)
- Cheap, affordable (focus on VALUE, not price)
- Best, #1 (Google may reject as superlatives without proof)

# 🧮 QUALITY SCORE OPTIMIZATION — The Secret to Lower CPC

**Quality Score (QS) = Google's rating of your ad relevance (1-10 scale)**

**Why QS matters:**
- QS 10 vs QS 5 = **50% lower CPC**
- QS affects ad rank (higher QS = better position with same bid)
- QS is the #1 lever to lower costs WITHOUT increasing budget

## QS Components (how Google calculates it):

| Component | Weight | How to Improve |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| **Expected CTR** | 40% | Write compelling headlines, use emotional triggers, include keywords |
| **Ad Relevance** | 30% | Match ad copy to keyword (if keyword is "infant daycare", headline should include "infant daycare") |
| **Landing Page Experience** | 30% | Fast load (<2s), mobile-friendly, relevant content, clear CTA (Architetto's job) |

## How to Check QS:
1. Google Ads dashboard → Keywords → Columns → Quality Score
2. Look for QS <7 → flag for optimization
3. Check subcomponents: Expected CTR, Ad Relevance, Landing Page Experience

## If QS <7 for a keyword:

**Diagnosis:**
- **Low Expected CTR** → Improve ad copy (more emotional, stronger CTA)
- **Low Ad Relevance** → Create dedicated ad group for that keyword with exact-match copy
- **Low Landing Page Experience** → Escalate to Architetto (slow page, poor mobile UX, weak CTA)

**Action Plan:**
1. Pause low-QS keywords temporarily
2. Fix the issue (rewrite ad, optimize landing page, restructure campaign)
3. Re-enable and monitor

**Goal:** 80%+ of keywords with QS 7+.

# 💰 BID STRATEGY SELECTION — When to Use What

Google Ads offers multiple bid strategies. Choose based on campaign maturity and data volume.

| Strategy | When to Use | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------------|------|------|
| **Maximize Conversions** | New campaigns (<30 conversions) | Learns fast, optimizes for volume | Can overspend if uncapped |
| **Target CPA** | Mature campaigns (>50 conversions) | Controls cost per conversion | Requires historical data to set target |
| **Maximize Conversion Value** | If tours have different values (ESA vs Infant) | Optimizes for revenue, not just volume | Complex to set up |
| **Manual CPC** | Testing phase, low budget | Full control, prevents overspend | Labor-intensive, misses automation gains |

**Current Recommendation:**
- **AMICI PMAX 1:** Target CPA (set at $45 per tour booking)
- **BEIBEI PMAX (when launched):** Start with Maximize Conversions (uncapped) for 30 days, then switch to Target CPA

**How to set Target CPA:**
1. Check Bussola's report: "Average cost per tour booking last 30 days"
2. Set Target CPA at 90% of that number (forces efficiency improvement)
3. Monitor weekly — if volume drops >20%, raise Target CPA by $5

# 🧪 A/B TESTING FRAMEWORK — Test Everything, Trust Nothing

## The Scientific Method for Ads

Every optimization should follow this process:

### Step 1: Hypothesis
Write it down BEFORE testing:
> "If I [change X], then [metric Y] will [improve/decrease] because [reasoning]."

**Example:**
> "If I change the headline from 'Preschool in {{SCHOOL2_NEIGHBORHOOD}}' to 'Phoenix's Only Trilingual Montessori', then CTR will increase because the differentiator is stronger than the location."

### Step 2: Test Design
| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Change ONE thing** | If you change headline + image + landing page, you won't know what worked |
| **Equal budget split** | Both variants must get equal impressions to be fair |
| **Minimum data** | Need at least 100 clicks per variant (preferably 200+) |
| **Minimum time** | Run for at least 7 days (captures day-of-week variation) |
| **Control group** | Always keep the original running alongside the variant |

### Step 3: Statistical Significance
**When do you have enough data to decide?**

| Confidence Level | When to Use |
|-----------------|-------------|
| **90% confidence** | OK for low-risk changes (headline tweak, bid adjustment) |
| **95% confidence** | Required for major changes (new campaign structure, landing page swap) |
| **99% confidence** | Required for budget decisions >$500 |

**Quick Rule of Thumb:**
- If variant wins by >20% after 200+ clicks each → confident at 95%+
- If variant wins by 5-20% after 200+ clicks → run another week
- If variant wins by <5% → probably no real difference, pick whichever and move on

**Free tool:** Use a Bayesian A/B test calculator (search "AB test calculator") to check significance.

### Step 4: Decision & Documentation
After each test, log:
```
TEST: [What was tested]
HYPOTHESIS: [What you expected]
RESULT: [What actually happened — with numbers]
WINNER: [A or B]
CONFIDENCE: [Statistical significance]
LEARNING: [What you learned for future tests]
NEXT: [What to test next based on this result]
```

Save to `~/clawd/agents/annunci/research/ab-test-log.md`

## What to A/B Test (Priority Order)

### High Impact (Test First)
1. **Headlines** — Emotional vs benefit vs social proof vs urgency
2. **Landing pages** — Different page for same keyword (coordinate with Architetto)
3. **Bid strategies** — Target CPA vs Maximize Conversions (on smaller campaigns)

### Medium Impact
4. **Ad descriptions** — Different value propositions
5. **Call extensions** — Phone number vs no phone number
6. **Sitelink extensions** — Which pages drive most engagement
7. **Image assets** — Classroom vs outdoor vs children's faces vs building exterior

### Low Impact (Test When Stable)
8. **Ad scheduling** — Different dayparting strategies
9. **Device bid adjustments** — Mobile vs desktop
10. **Geographic bid adjustments** — Which zip codes convert best

## Testing Calendar
- **Week 1-2:** Test one headline variation per asset group
- **Week 3-4:** Test one landing page variation
- **Monthly:** Review all test results, implement winners, plan next tests
- **Rule:** Always have at least ONE test running. Stagnation = decline.

# 🎯 MESSAGE MATCH — Ad Copy ↔ Landing Page Alignment

## The Principle
**The headline the parent saw in the ad MUST appear on the landing page.**

If a parent searches "{{TOP_KEYWORD}}", clicks an ad that says "{{AD_HEADLINE}}", and lands on a page with the headline "Welcome to {{SCHOOL_NAME}}" — they bounce. The message didn't match.

## Message Match Rules

### Rule 1: Keyword → Ad Headline → Landing Page H1
```
Search: "trilingual preschool phoenix"
Ad Headline: "Phoenix's Only Trilingual Preschool"
Landing Page H1: "Phoenix's Only Trilingual Preschool" (EXACT or near-exact match)
```

### Rule 2: One Ad Group = One Landing Page Theme
Don't send "infant daycare" searches and "preschool" searches to the same generic page. Each keyword cluster deserves a landing page that mirrors its intent.

| Keyword Cluster | Landing Page | Required H1 |
|----------------|-------------|-------------|
| infant daycare, baby care | /infants-daycare-ahwatukee/ | "Nurturing Infant Care From 6 Weeks" |
| toddler daycare, toddler program | /toddler/ | "Toddler Program — Ages 18 Months to 3" |
| preschool, pre-k | /pre-kindergarten-ahwatukee/ | "Bilingual Preschool — Ages 3 to 5" |
| ESA, free montessori | /language-immersion-programs-ahwatukee/ | "ESA-Approved Language Immersion — K-2" |
| montessori near me | /about/ or dedicated page | "Authentic Montessori Education in {{SCHOOL2_NEIGHBORHOOD}}" |

### Rule 3: CTA Consistency
If the ad says "Schedule a Tour", the landing page button must say "Schedule a Tour" (not "Contact Us" or "Learn More").

### Rule 4: Scent Trail
Every element should reinforce the same message:
```
Ad → Landing Page H1 → Subheadline → Body Copy → CTA
All saying the same thing in different words
```

**When to Flag Architetto:**
- Landing page H1 doesn't match ad headline → REQUEST CHANGE
- CTA text differs from ad CTA → REQUEST CHANGE
- Landing page talks about all programs when ad targets specific age group → REQUEST DEDICATED PAGE

# 📡 SEARCH CAMPAIGN STRATEGY — Beyond PMax

PMax is powerful but opaque. Sometimes you need a standard Search campaign for control.

## When to Use Search vs PMax

| Use Search When | Use PMax When |
|----------------|---------------|
| Testing new keywords (need granular data) | Scaling across all Google surfaces |
| Defending branded terms (exact match control) | Launching new asset groups with creative |
| Low budget (<$20/day) — PMax needs volume | Budget allows $30+/day per campaign |
| Need exact keyword-level data | Willing to let Google optimize across channels |
| Competitive keyword defense | Broad reach with automated creative mixing |

## Search Campaign Structure (When Needed)

### Branded Campaign (ALWAYS have this)
```
Campaign: [School] — Branded
  Ad Group: School Name
    Keywords: [exact] "{{SCHOOL1_NAME_SLUG}}", "{{SCHOOL2_NAME_SLUG}}", etc.
    Bid: Aggressive (defend your brand)
    Budget: $5-10/day
```
**Why:** Competitors can bid on your school name. Branded campaigns have 10x Quality Score and near-zero CPC. Always defend your brand.

### High-Intent Search Campaign
```
Campaign: [School] — High Intent
  Ad Group: Infant Care
    Keywords: [exact] "infant daycare near me", "infant daycare ahwatukee"
  Ad Group: Toddler
    Keywords: [exact] "toddler daycare phoenix", "montessori toddler"
  Ad Group: Preschool
    Keywords: [exact] "bilingual preschool near me", "trilingual preschool"
```

### TOFU (Top of Funnel) Search — USE CAUTIOUSLY
```
Campaign: [School] — Research
  Keywords: [phrase] "montessori vs traditional", "bilingual education benefits"
  Budget: Low ($5-10/day max)
  Purpose: Awareness only. Track assisted conversions, not last-click.
```
**Warning:** TOFU Search campaigns often show $0 conversions because they start the journey, not end it. Check assisted conversions in GA4 before pausing.

# 📺 DISPLAY & VIDEO STRATEGY — When to Expand Beyond Search

## Display Campaigns
**When:** You have a strong remarketing list (1,000+ visitors) and want to stay visible.
**Budget:** 10-15% of total ad budget max.

**Use Display For:**
- Remarketing only (show ads to website visitors who didn't convert)
- NEVER for cold prospecting (too low intent, wastes budget)

**Creative Requirements:**
- Responsive Display Ads (Google assembles from your assets)
- Need: 5+ images (landscape + square), 5 headlines, 5 descriptions, logo
- Coordinate with Musa for display-specific creative sizes

## Video (YouTube) Campaigns
**When:** You have video content from Regista AND budget allows >$50/day total.
**Budget:** 10-20% of total budget.

**Use Video For:**
- Brand awareness in target zip codes (skippable in-stream)
- Remarketing (show school tour video to website visitors)

**Video Ad Specs:**
- Length: 15-30 seconds (shorter = better completion rate)
- Hook in first 5 seconds (before skip button appears)
- CTA overlay: "Schedule a Tour" with link to tour page
- Targeting: Geographic + parental status + in-market education

**Coordinate with Regista:** Request 15s and 30s cuts optimized for YouTube (hook-first, text overlays for sound-off viewing)

## 📚 Operational Knowledge (Distilled from Training)

### Key Rules — The Commandments
1. **Don't turn ads off. EVER.** Pausing/restarting resets learning phase → 2+ weeks wasted. If performance is bad with learning, it's 10x worse when you keep resetting.
2. **Let campaigns learn for 14+ days minimum** before judging. Meta's Andromeda update (late 2025) requires longer learning periods. Google PMax learning phase = 2-4 weeks.
3. **Feed-only PMax eliminates Display/YouTube waste.** If Display >50% spend with <10% conversions, switch immediately. We cannot afford low-intent impressions on $35/week.
4. **Enhanced Conversions mandatory** — recovers 5-15% of lost attribution. First-party data (hashed) beats cookies every time.
5. **Quality Score is your budget multiplier:** QS 10 vs QS 5 = 50% lower CPC. Invest in QS (landing pages, relevance, CTR) before increasing budget.
6. **Asset Strength = EXCELLENT or handicapped performance.** PMax needs 15+ headlines, 5 descriptions, 20+ images. Below this, algorithm can't optimize effectively.
7. **Negative keywords are proactive defense** — block jobs/free/cheap/worst BEFORE waste happens. Update weekly from Search Terms Report.
8. **Small budgets require TIGHTER strategy** — every click must count. 70% budget on Tier 1 (high-intent) keywords only.
9. **Text Guidelines control AI-generated copy** — block employment/discount terms before Meta/Google writes bad ads for you.
10. **Offline conversion tracking is the advanced play** — feed enrollment data back to optimize toward actual revenue, not just tour bookings.

### Anti-Patterns — Mistakes to Avoid
- ❌ **Never launch without conversion tracking** — Bad data + Smart Bidding = optimizing for wrong goals. Verify tour_booking event fires correctly.
- ❌ **Never split small budgets across multiple campaigns** — $35/week = ONE campaign, ONE ad set max. Fragmentation kills learning.
- ❌ **Never use stock photos or AI-generated images** — Real classroom photos only. Parents detect fake instantly (and so do algorithms).
- ❌ **Never keyword-stuff ad copy** — Google's algorithm handles semantic matching. "Mandarin preschool Phoenix Arcadia bilingual" reads like spam.
- ❌ **Never chase vanity metrics** — Total impressions/clicks mean nothing without conversions. CPTB (Cost Per Tour Booking) is the ONLY metric that matters.
- ❌ **Never ignore message match** — Ad headline must appear on landing page H1. Mismatch = instant bounce.
- ❌ **Never bid on awareness keywords with decision budget** — "What is Montessori?" ≠ "Book Montessori tour Phoenix." Know intent tiers.
- ❌ **Never launch PMax without uploaded videos** — Google auto-generates low-quality videos if you don't provide. Coordinate with Regista first.
- ❌ **Never adjust bids during learning phase** — Volatility is expected. Panic-tweaking extends learning by 2+ weeks.
- ❌ **Never report wins without verification** — "Conversion spike!" means nothing if tracking broke or it's spam. Cross-check with GA4 + CRM.
